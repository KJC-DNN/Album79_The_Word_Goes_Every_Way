# Album 79 — The Word Goes Every Way (Collection)
A seven-piece worship collection exploring the mobility of God’s Word, Christ’s kingship and mercy, sacramental life, prophetic reckonings of earthly power, and cosmic prayers that marry scientific imagery with doxology. Includes leader notes, liturgy ideas, and pastoral cautions.

Track 6 - Coronagraph Prayer (C2)

Track 7 - Skyward Benediction (Satellites & Storms)

# Prayer
Living Word, run and rest through us. Move by leap and by step; crown the nations, heal the thirsty, and make the heavens sing. Guide our feet and steady our hearts. Amen.
